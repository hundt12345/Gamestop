01
SEP 2020OWNERSHIP / SIGNAL

The activist entry.

Ryan Cohen builds a stake in GameStop. The retailer is still being priced like a problem; a different reading of the business starts to circulate.

RC enters the frame
SEC filings ↗IR archive ↗
02
NOV 2020STRATEGY / LETTER

A letter, not a whisper.

Cohen's open letter to the board turns a position into a thesis: customer obsession, a stronger digital layer and a business that has to move faster.

read the thesis
GameStop IR ↗filing trail ↗
03
JAN 2021WSB / CONVICTION

DFV keeps showing the receipts.

Keith Gill, posting as DeepFuckingValue and Roaring Kitty, has been documenting the trade for months. WallStreetBets turns the screenshots into a shared language.

posts become evidence
Roaring Kitty ↗r / wallstreetbets ↗
04
27 JAN 2021MARKET / BREAKOUT

The squeeze goes vertical.

Short interest, options flow, momentum and a meme-native crowd collide. The line stops behaving like a normal company chart.

the peak enters the feed
price history ↗SEC spotlight ↗
05
28 JAN 2021PLATFORM / FRICTION

The buy button goes dark.

Brokerage restrictions arrive at the moment of maximum attention. “Why can't I buy?” becomes a product question, a market-structure question and a cultural flashpoint.

access is part of the story
Robinhood help ↗SEC testimony ↗
06
FEB 2021HEARING / RECORD

The hearing enters the record.

Congress hears from Keith Gill, Robinhood, Citadel and Melvin Capital. The livestream turns a trading episode into a primary-source archive.

watch the room
House hearing ↗video search ↗
07
JUN 2021COMMUNITY / DD

Superstonk builds an archive.

After the first wave, the community begins to catalogue market mechanics, filings and counter-arguments. The vocabulary shifts from hype to due diligence.

read before you react
DD index ↗GMEDD ↗
08
JUL 2022CAPITAL / STRUCTURE

A split dividend, a new denominator.

The four-for-one split changes the unit everyone is looking at. A corporate action becomes another chapter in the community's forensic reading of filings.

read the release
GameStop IR ↗SEC EDGAR ↗
09
MAR 2023EARNINGS / BUSINESS

Profit changes the texture.

Quarterly reports pull the focus back to the business: cash, inventory, operating costs and the question of what a transformed retailer can become.

numbers over narrative

MAY — JUN 2024RETURN / VOLATILITY

The return of Roaring Kitty.

A single post after a long silence is enough to restart the internet's oldest conversation. The company responds with filings, offerings and a market that moves in public.
